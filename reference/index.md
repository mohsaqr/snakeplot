# Package index

## Overview

- [`snakeplot`](https://pak.dynasite.org/Snakeplot/reference/snakeplot-package.md)
  [`snakeplot-package`](https://pak.dynasite.org/Snakeplot/reference/snakeplot-package.md)
  : snakeplot: Serpentine Plots for Long Timeline, Sequential and Survey
  Data

## Survey and experience sampling responses

One band per item, or one band per study day for beep-level ratings;
responses as ticks, dots or stacked bars.

- [`survey_snake()`](https://pak.dynasite.org/Snakeplot/reference/survey_snake.md)
  : Plot survey responses as a serpentine band per item
- [`survey_sequence()`](https://pak.dynasite.org/Snakeplot/reference/survey_sequence.md)
  : Plot survey responses as a serpentine of stacked bars
- [`sequential_dist()`](https://pak.dynasite.org/Snakeplot/reference/sequential_dist.md)
  : Plot unipolar survey responses as a serpentine of single-hue stacked
  bars

## Sequences and timelines

Ordered categorical states, several sequences at once, and phases with
start and end dates.

- [`sequence_snake()`](https://pak.dynasite.org/Snakeplot/reference/sequence_snake.md)
  : Plot a long state sequence as blocks along a serpentine path
- [`multi_snake()`](https://pak.dynasite.org/Snakeplot/reference/multi_snake.md)
  : Plot many state sequences as a serpentine index or distribution plot
- [`timeline_snake()`](https://pak.dynasite.org/Snakeplot/reference/timeline_snake.md)
  : Plot a timeline of phases as a serpentine of monthly blocks

## Daily activity

Time-stamped events and continuous signals on a daily clock, one band
per day.

- [`activity_snake()`](https://pak.dynasite.org/Snakeplot/reference/activity_snake.md)
  : Plot daily activity as a serpentine timeline of days
- [`line_snake()`](https://pak.dynasite.org/Snakeplot/reference/line_snake.md)
  : Plot a continuous series as a line through a serpentine of days

## Panels

One serpentine plot per group.

- [`facet_snake()`](https://pak.dynasite.org/Snakeplot/reference/facet_snake.md)
  : Draw one snake plot per group in a grid of panels

## Results

The tables of a drawn plot’s layout.

- [`as.data.frame(`*`<snake_layout>`*`)`](https://pak.dynasite.org/Snakeplot/reference/as.data.frame.snake_layout.md)
  : Extract the tables of a serpentine plot's layout

## Palettes

Diverging and sequential colors, interpolated to the number of levels.

- [`snake_palette()`](https://pak.dynasite.org/Snakeplot/reference/snake_palette.md)
  : Get a built-in palette for snake plots
- [`snake_palettes`](https://pak.dynasite.org/Snakeplot/reference/snake_palettes.md)
  : Built-in color palettes for snake plots

## Data

A 14-day experience sampling study of university students.

- [`ema_emotions`](https://pak.dynasite.org/Snakeplot/reference/ema_emotions.md)
  : Person-level emotion ratings from an experience-sampling study
- [`student_survey`](https://pak.dynasite.org/Snakeplot/reference/student_survey.md)
  : Person-level ratings of emotion, motivation, regulation and
  engagement
- [`ema_beeps`](https://pak.dynasite.org/Snakeplot/reference/ema_beeps.md)
  : Prompt-level happiness and anger ratings from an experience-sampling
  study
